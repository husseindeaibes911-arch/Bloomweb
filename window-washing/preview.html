Our Services

Everything your windows & gutters need

One trusted local crew for the exterior details that make a home look cared for. Here's exactly what we do — and how we do it right.

Window Washing

Our water-fed pole system brushes away every bit of build-up on the glass, frames and seals, then rinses everything down with pure filtered water. Because the water is mineral-free, your windows dry perfectly clear — no streaks, no spots, and no ladders leaning against your home.

Book this service

What's included

  • Build-up brushed off glass, frames & seals
  • Pure filtered-water rinse — no streaks, no spots
  • Reaches high windows safely from the ground
  • Interior & exterior options available

Gutter Cleaning

We scoop out every bit of debris and bag it up — we never blow it all over your house and yard and leave a mess behind. Then we hose the gutters clean and make sure the drains and downspouts are working properly. If a downspout is clogged, we disassemble the pole, use the proper tool to clear whatever is stuck inside, and reassemble it so water flows the way it should.

Book this service

What's included

  • All debris hand-scooped & bagged — no mess
  • Gutters flushed clean with water
  • Drains & downspouts checked for proper flow
  • Clogged downspouts dismantled, cleared & reassembled

Exterior Gutter Whitening

Offered separately from a full gutter cleaning, this service focuses on the outside face of your gutters — removing the grimy black streaks (tiger stripes) so they look bright, white and spotless from the ground. It's a great add-on to a cleaning, or a stand-alone way to boost your home's curb appeal.

What's included

  • Removes streaks & grime from the gutter face
  • Restores that clean, bright curb-appeal look
  • Great add-on with a full gutter cleaning
  • Available as a stand-alone service

Home Gutter Guards
Gutter Guards

Three guard options, professionally installed

Keep leaves and debris out for good. We'll help you choose the right one for your home and budget.

Foam

Budget-friendly

Porous foam inserts sit inside the gutter and block debris while letting water pass through. Simple and cost-effective.

  • Lowest-cost option
  • Quick, no-fuss installation
  • Blocks leaves & larger debris
Popular

PVC Mesh

Net-style screen

A durable net-like mesh that clips over the gutter, keeping out leaves and fine debris while water flows straight through.

  • Fine mesh blocks small debris
  • Great balance of price & performance
  • Lightweight & long-lasting

Aluminum

Premium & durable

Rigid aluminum guards are the most durable choice — they won't rust or sag and stand up to Montreal winters year after year.

  • Strongest, longest-lasting option
  • Rust-proof & weather-resistant
  • Best protection for heavy leaf areas

Protect your home

Expert fit

We assess your roof and leaf load and install the right guard properly — no guesswork.

Our Story

Built on doing the job right

HD Ultra Exterior started with a simple idea: exterior cleaning should be done thoroughly and tidily, with real pride in the result. Too many homeowners were used to streaky windows and gutter crews that blew debris all over the yard.

So we invested in a proper water-fed pole system for spotless, filtered-water results, and we clean gutters the careful way — scooping and bagging every bit of debris, checking that downspouts flow, and clearing them when they don't. More than 500 Montreal homes later, that approach is still what sets us apart.
