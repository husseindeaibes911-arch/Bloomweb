Our Services

Everything your windows & gutters need

One trusted local crew for the exterior details that make a home look cared for.

Window Washing

Our water-fed pole system brushes away every bit of build-up on the glass, frames and seals, then rinses with pure filtered water so the panes dry perfectly clear.

  • Build-up brushed off glass, frames & seals
  • Pure filtered-water rinse — no streaks, no spots
  • Reaches high windows safely from the ground
  • Interior & exterior options available

Gutter Cleaning

We scoop out every bit of debris and bag it up — we never blow it all over your house and yard. Then we hose the gutters clean and make sure water flows freely.

  • All debris hand-scooped & bagged — no mess
  • Gutters flushed clean with water
  • Drains & downspouts checked for proper flow
  • Clogged downspouts dismantled, cleared & reassembled

Gutter Guard Installation

Stop the clogs before they start. We supply and install three types of gutter guards so leaves and debris stay out and your gutters keep flowing.

  • Three options: foam, PVC mesh & aluminum
  • Professional supply & installation
  • Fewer cleanings, less overflow
  • Guidance on the right fit for your roof

Exterior Gutter Whitening

Offered separately — we clean the outside face of your gutters so they look bright and spotless from the ground, removing those grimy black tiger stripes.

  • Removes streaks & grime from the gutter face
  • Restores that clean, bright curb-appeal look
  • Great add-on with a full gutter cleaning
  • Available as a stand-alone service
How We Work

Careful, tidy & done right

A methodical process that protects your home and leaves nothing behind but shine.

Brush & loosen

The water-fed pole brush lifts all the build-up off the glass, frames and seals.

Pure-water rinse

We rinse everything down with filtered water so it dries with zero streaks or spots.

Scoop & bag

Gutter debris is scooped out and bagged — never blown onto your house or yard.

Flush & clear

We hose it all down and confirm drains flow — clogged downspouts are opened up, cleared with a tool, and reassembled.

Gutter Guards

Three guard options, professionally installed

Keep leaves and debris out for good. We'll help you choose the right one for your home and budget.

Foam

Budget-friendly

Porous foam inserts sit inside the gutter and block debris while letting water pass through. Simple and cost-effective.

  • Lowest-cost option
  • Quick, no-fuss installation
  • Blocks leaves & larger debris
Popular

PVC Mesh

Net-style screen

A durable net-like mesh that clips over the gutter, keeping out leaves and fine debris while water flows straight through.

  • Fine mesh blocks small debris
  • Great balance of price & performance
  • Lightweight & long-lasting

Aluminum

Premium & durable

Rigid aluminum guards are the most durable choice — they won't rust or sag and stand up to Montreal winters year after year.

  • Strongest, longest-lasting option
  • Rust-proof & weather-resistant
  • Best protection for heavy leaf areas
